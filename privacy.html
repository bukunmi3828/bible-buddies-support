Privacy Policy

Bible Buddies is designed for children, but every account belongs to a parent or guardian. We use only the information needed to provide profiles, learning content, playback, progress and optional premium features. We do not sell personal information, show behavioural advertising, provide public profiles or offer child-to-child messaging.

Information we handle

For the parent account, we handle an email address, display name, consent record and account security information. For a child profile, we handle a nickname, age band, chosen avatar and interests. We also store learning activity such as playback position, favourites, completed audio, memory-verse progress and quiz results. If a parent subscribes, we handle an account identifier, product identifier and subscription entitlement status; Apple processes the payment details. We do not request a child's email address, exact birth date, school, home address or precise location.

How information is used

Information is used to operate and secure the account, personalise age-appropriate content, synchronise progress, provide parent reports, validate premium access, prevent misuse and maintain the service. Local Bible Time reminders are stored and scheduled on the device.

Service providers

Account and learning records are processed using Supabase. Apple processes App Store purchases and RevenueCat validates subscription status and premium access. Audio is stored in Backblaze B2 and delivered using short-lived authorised links. Expo services are used to build and distribute the mobile application. These providers process limited information needed to operate and protect the service.

Sharing and advertising

We share information only with service providers acting for Bible Buddies, when required by law, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. Providers are not permitted to use child information for behavioural advertising.

Retention and security

We retain account, progress and subscription-status information while the account is active and only as reasonably needed afterward for security, legal or operational purposes. We use access controls, database row-level security and protected media links. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Parent rights and choices

A parent or guardian may request access, correction, export or deletion of account and child-profile information. Account and child-profile deletion are available inside the app. Subscription billing can be managed through the Apple Account. Requests may also be sent to bukunmiakinfenwa@gmail.com.

Children

Children should use Bible Buddies with a parent or guardian. If you believe information was collected from a child without appropriate permission, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed where required.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or law changes. Material changes will be communicated to the parent and renewed consent will be requested where required.

Effective: 21 July 2026
